Clinical Applications

Cross-specialty clinical applications

How it is used across medical specialties.

Dermatology

Chronic spontaneous urticaria, contact dermatitis, eczema/atopic dermatitis flares, acute pruritus, and insect bite reactions.

Otolaryngology (ENT)

Seasonal and perennial allergic rhinitis, allergic rhinosinusitis, and post-nasal drip.

Pulmonology / Allergy & Immunology

Allergic asthma flares (as adjunct therapy for upper airway symptom management), pollen/dust/pet allergen sensitivities, and systemic allergic reactions.

Ophthalmology

Allergic conjunctivitis (often combined with topical ocular drops for systemic relief).

Pediatrics

Allergic rhinitis, pediatric eczema, and pediatric urticaria (age-appropriate liquid/syrup formulations).

General / Internal Medicine & Emergency Medicine

Acute mild-to-moderate allergic responses, food/drug hypersensitivity reactions (non-anaphylactic), and general symptom control.
