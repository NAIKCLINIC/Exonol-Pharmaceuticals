A Multifaceted Antibiotic

EDOX-LB (Doxycycline combined with Lactic Acid Bacillus) is an oral broad-spectrum tetracycline antibiotic paired with a probiotic spore-former to prevent antibiotic-associated diarrhea and maintain intestinal flora during treatment.

Clinical Applications

Cross-specialty clinical applications

How it is used across medical specialties.

Dermatology

  • Acne Vulgaris: Moderate-to-severe inflammatory acne (systemic control of Cutibacterium acnes).
  • Rosacea: Papulopustular rosacea (anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effect).
  • Hidradenitis Suppurativa & Bacterial Folliculitis: Long-term low-dose/standard immunomodulation and infection control.

Venereology & Urology / Sexual Health

  • Non-Gonococcal Urethritis (NGU) & Cervicitis: Primary treatment for Chlamydia trachomatis and Mycoplasma genitalium.
  • Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID): Outpatient empirical regimens (combined with metronidazole/ceftriaxone).
  • Syphilis & Lymphogranuloma Venereum: Alternative regimen in penicillin-allergic patients.
  • Chronic Bacterial Prostatitis: Deep tissue penetration for susceptible organisms.

Pulmonology & Respiratory Medicine

  • Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP): Atypical coverage (Mycoplasma pneumoniae, Chlamydia pneumoniae, Legionella).
  • Acute Exacerbation of Chronic Bronchitis / COPD: Empirical coverage for upper and lower respiratory pathogens.

Infectious Diseases & Vector-Borne Illnesses

  • Rickettsial Infections: Scrub typhus, Rocky Mountain spotted fever, and epidemic typhus.
  • Spirochetal & Zoonotic Infections: Leptospirosis (treatment and prophylaxis), Lyme disease (Borrelia burgdorferi), and Brucellosis (in combination with rifampin/aminoglycosides).
  • Malaria: Prophylaxis and treatment (in combination with artemisinin derivatives/quinine) for Plasmodium falciparum.

Ophthalmology & ENT

  • Ocular Rosacea & Meibomian Gland Dysfunction (MGD): Systemic anti-inflammatory control to improve tear film quality.
  • Trachoma: Secondary management of ocular Chlamydia trachomatis.
  • Acute Sinusitis: Alternative choice for β-lactam-allergic patients.

Gastroenterology & General Medicine

  • Intestinal Amebiasis & Cholera: Adjunctive treatment for severe watery diarrhea or parasitic infections.
  • Periodontitis (Dental/Oral Surgery): Adjunctive systemic therapy for progressive adult periodontitis.
