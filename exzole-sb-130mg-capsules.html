Clinical Applications

Cross-specialty clinical applications

How it is used across medical specialties.

Dermatology & Venereology

  • Dermatophytosis: Recurrent or extensive Tinea corporis, Tinea cruris, Tinea pedis, and Tinea manuum.
  • Onychomycosis: Fungal nail infections of fingernails and toenails (pulse or continuous therapy).
  • Pityriasis Versicolor & Cutaneous Candidiasis: Extensive or recalcitrant cutaneous fungal infections.

General / Internal Medicine & Infectious Diseases

  • Systemic & Endemic Mycoses: Histoplasmosis, Blastomycosis, and Aspergillosis (non-immunocompromised and mild-to-moderate immunocompromised patients).
  • Empirical & Maintenance Antifungal Therapy: Prophylaxis or treatment in neutropenic or immunocompromised hosts.

Pulmonology

  • Allergic Bronchopulmonary Aspergillosis (ABPA): Adjunctive treatment to corticosteroids to reduce fungal load in the airways.
  • Chronic Pulmonary Aspergillosis (CPA): Long-term suppressive management.

Gynecology

  • Recurrent Vulvovaginal Candidiasis (RVVC): Systemic management when topical azoles or single-dose fluconazole fail.

ENT (Otorhinolaryngology)

  • Fungal Rhinosinusitis: Adjunctive systemic antifungal cover for allergic fungal rhinosinusitis (AFRS) or invasive fungal sinusitis post-debridement.
